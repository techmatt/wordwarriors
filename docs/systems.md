# Systems: where each thing starts

| System | Entry point | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | `src/main.ts` → `src/scenes/BootScene.ts` | Loads every art id in `content/art/assets.ts`, makes particle textures, reads the voice manifest, handles `?scene=`. |
| Episode flow | `src/episode/Episode.ts` | The step list (bedroom → entry hall warm-up → house 1 → battle 1 → house 2 → battle 2 → victory); `advance(scene)` starts the next step's scene. Inside a house step the hub and its rooms hand over with `enterRoom(scene, slot)` / `backToHub(scene, from)`. `castEpisode()` draws the cast (`src/rules/cast.ts`) and the quest place at each episode's start; `waker()` is the dad who wakes her, `helper()` (the ally, or the waker) gives the gift word, fills `{helper}` and leads the quest, `narrator()` is the helper's person (whose voice the instructions are in); `castId(id)` / `castArt(id)` / `castFace(id)` turn a person into the form they play this episode. `episode.story` (`drawStory`, `src/rules/story.ts`, drawn with the cast) picks the helper's quest line (`questSetupLine()`) and hub scene (`hubScene()`); `storyWords()` is every word they say, kept out of every draw. `giftWord()` draws the gift word on first need (`drawGift`, kept in `episode.gift`). `movePlan()`, `housePlan()` (the episode's three `RoomPlan`s) and `grabPlan()` are each made once, in that order of dependence. `episode.fetched` (key words, in order) and `episode.gift` feed the battle. Cast options from the URL: `setCastOptions` (BootScene `castOptions`); the fixed default episode is `EPISODE_DEFAULTS` (`src/config.ts`). Parked v0.1 kinds (`alarm`, `prep`, `retreat`, `spell`) are reachable only by `jumpTo`. |
| Input | `src/input/Controls.ts` | Create one per scene in `create()`; call `update()` first and `endFrame()` last in `update()`. `virtualPad` is the test input. |
| Spelling keys | `src/input/TextEntry.ts` | `openTextEntry(fn)` while a spelling scene is open; returns the closer. |
| Voice data | `src/voice/VoiceBank.ts` | `voiceBank.loadGroups([...])`; groups are `core`, `phonics`, `nar_<narrator>`, `fighter_<fighter>`, `end_<person>` (victory scene lines). |
| Voice arbiter | `src/audio/VoiceBus.ts` | `voiceBus.say(id, 'instruction' \| 'bark', anchor?)`. The only way to speak. Caption and Bubbles listen to it. |
| SFX | `src/audio/sfx.ts` | Synthesised; one master gain, ducked while a voice line plays. |
| Music | `src/audio/music.ts` | Synthesised step sequencer: `startMusic('title' \| 'room' \| 'battle')`, ducked under speech. |
| Victory scene | `src/scenes/EndingScene.ts`, `src/rules/endings.ts`, `content/house/endings.ts` | The victory step starts here (`SCENE_FOR.victory` is `Ending`), then goes on to `Victory`. `pickEnding(cast, progress.lastEnding(), rand)` once per episode (`episode.ending`; `?ending=<id>` fixes it); `endingPeople(cast)` (the hero first, the villain last) stand across `stand`; `endingScript(cast, id)` is the four beats, each `{ who, line }`, said as barks over `who` (a dropped bark is tried again). `hookState.ending` for tests. Voice groups `end_<person>` are in `episodeGroups()`. |
| Journey map | `src/scenes/JourneyScene.ts` | Shown by `advance()` before battle and victory steps (`JOURNEY_BEFORE`); `?nojourney` skips it. |
| Frame safety net | `src/main.ts` | `Phaser.Game.prototype.step` wrapped so one throwing frame cannot stop the loop. |
| Dialogue box | `src/ui/Caption.ts` | Instruction lines (and barks with no anchor, e.g. a taunt heard from afar), spoken word highlighted, beside the speaker's framed face and name on their side with a tail. Top or bottom, whichever covers less of the scene's `avoid()` keep-outs (rooms: `RoomScene.keepOut()`, spots, her, the banner, the scroll); `track(id, where)` puts a face on the side the speaker stands and a glow at their feet; `mode(u)` per line: `show`, `portrait` (face only: the sentence banner and recap cards light their own words) or `hide`. `compact` in battle: small and always at the bottom. |
| Speaker portraits | `src/ui/Portrait.ts` | `speakerOf(u)` (`phonics` = Dada Matt, or the waker while he is the villain; a line's speaker is a person, and the face is the form they play), `makePortrait(scene, speaker, size)`: the name-free face card (evil: the top of the body art) framed in the speaker's palette colour, name plate under it. |
| Bubbles | `src/ui/Bubbles.ts` | Barks over a moving anchor (`{x, y, bubbleOffset, active}`), e.g. a `Fighter`, with a small face chip; the tail follows the speaker even when the bubble is held in from an edge. |
| Text fitting | `src/ui/fitText.ts` | `fitText(text, w, h?)`: wraps between words, then shrinks until it fits. Every text box that can run long uses it (bubbles, battle banner, `floatText`, recap cards, grown-ups page); the caption and the sentence banner lay out rows of words themselves. `__ww.texts()` lists visible texts with an `offscreen` flag (`tests/text.spec.ts`). |
| Walking rooms | `src/world/RoomScene.ts` | Base for picks, the bedroom, the hub, the parked games. Subclass: `background()`, `build()`, `choose(spot)`, optional `hint()`. A spot with `correct: true` is what F4 walks to. |
| Bedroom | `src/scenes/BedroomScene.ts` | The wake-up. Plays `BEDROOMS[0]` (`content/house/house.ts`): the waker (`waker()`: the primary dad in his form) gives the `wake_*` lines, the secondary dad comes in too (`wake_<dad>_hi` half the time), the helper says the quest's setup line (`questSetupLine()`), the villain's mood reveals, each `BedroomObject` does its `reaction` + `sound` on green, the door sparkles after `DOOR_SPARKLE_MS`. No text, no reading. |
| Hub | `src/scenes/HubScene.ts` | Phases `intro → circle → doors → secret → front → leaving`. Part 1: the helper's story scene (`hubScene()`, `teach()`): its lines, then its `prop` (an art id or an emoji card) pops up with the gift word on it, the last line trails off into `nar_<narrator>_gift_word_<w>`, the scene's `circle` line sends her to the glowing circle (yellow repeats it), the word flies into her, the scene's `done` line; then doors `a` and `b`. Part 2: the secret `counter` door (the attic). A door is invisible until it is usable: the side doors pop in after the gift, the front door when the part's rooms are done, the counter door in part 2. Restarted on each return, so all state is read back from `episode`. Its backdrop is `hubArt(episode.place)` (the house's `room_hub` or a quest place's `room_place_<id>`); away from home the way out is `prop_portal` and the lines are the `away_*` ones. |
| Minigame pool | `src/rules/minigames.ts` | Pure. `MINIGAMES` (id, scene, weight, slots), `drawGames(store, rand)`: two fetch games and a counter game per episode, weighted, no repeat; `enterRoom` starts the drawn game's scene. The sentence game in each frame, and the word machine (fetch rooms only). `episodePlans` (`src/rules/sentences.ts`) turns the draw into `RoomPlan`s (`kind: 'sentence' | 'machine'`, each with `key` and `fetches`); `?game=` / `?games=sentence` override the draw (`roomGames` in Episode). |
| Word machine | `src/scenes/WordMachine.ts`, `src/rules/wordMachine.ts`, `content/phonics/families.ts` | A `Minigame` for a fetch room whose plan is `kind: 'machine'`. `planMachine(store, { avoid, taken })`: `rounds` words from the word bag, one family then another, leaning to shaky `rimes`, each word by the bank's `wordWeight` × its shaky `onsets`; make mode builds only a family's pictured `words`. Per round: picture, rime, tiles (`onNear` says the letter sound); green → `letter_<x>_sound`, a beat, `rime_<r>`; right → payoff, `word_<w>`, picture into the `Bag`, `episode.fetched`/`read`, `progress.record` for `words`, `rimes`, `onsets`, bag spend; wrong → `machine_makes` + `silly_<w>` + `machine_silly_N`, the tile pops back. Hooks: `room.machine`. |
| Charge the Spell | `src/scenes/ChargeRoom.ts`, `src/rules/charge.ts`, `content/phonics/runes.ts` | Item 053; one word a visit since 058; no presses per rune since 059. A `Minigame` (`CHARGE_GAME`, fetch rooms only) whose plan is `kind: 'charge'` (`key` and `fetches` the word, a battle item). `planCharge(store, { avoid }, rand)`: one word of `CHARGE.units` units from `CHARGE_WORDS` (familiar pictured words whose every unit is a rune, no sound twice) through `drawWords`, × its sounds' tier gate (tier 1 continuous always; tiers 2, 3 open as the one before firms, `CHARGE.tiers`) × its sounds' shakiness; `episodePlans` keeps it off every other room's words. Its runes are the word's sounds, in order. Per rune (item 059, no presses): it lights, the helper's gesture and `letter_<x>_sound`, a `CHARGE.sayBackMs` beat for her to say it back (held under dad lock), and it charges. Then the read, the room's one press: Dad's yes (key 2, live from the moment the word lights) says `word_<w>`; a no (key 1) or `CHARGE.readTimeoutMs` sounds it out together once first; either way `words` and the word's `sounds` are recorded (right with his yes), the bag taken from or returned to, and the word goes to `read` and `fetched`. The help-level ramp (`chargeLevel`, ProgressStore `chargeLevels`) is kept in the store but unused since item 059. Charged sounds go to `episode.charged` (`{ sounds, part }`); BattleScene `useCharged` fires each rune's `move` after the fetched items, with a rune chip row in the HUD. Forced into door a by `FORCE_PHONICS_ROOM` / `?room=charge` (`roomGames` in Episode). Hooks: `room.charge`, `battle.runesFired`. |
| Stretchy Bridge | `src/scenes/BridgeRoom.ts`, `src/rules/bridge.ts`, `src/audio/holdSound.ts` | Item 054. A `Minigame` (`BRIDGE_GAME`, fetch rooms only) whose plan is `kind: 'bridge'` (`fetches` its words, `key` the last). `planBridge(store, { avoid, taken }, rand)`: `BRIDGE.words` from `BRIDGE_WORDS` (familiar short-vowel things) by `drawWords` × tier gate (`bridgeTier`: 1 hold-sound CVC, 2 stop CVC, 3 blends and digraphs; a tier opens as the one before reaches level 2, `BRIDGE.tiers`) × 2 in her word bag, easier tiers first; `bridgePictures`: the far bank's others, sharing two sounds from the start, then one, then the rime (`sharedStart`). Per word: the help level (`bridgeLevel`, ProgressStore `bridgeLevels`); stones by sound unit over the gap (`gapFor`), the stick slowed on them (`RoomScene.moveScale`), each stone's `letter_<x>_sound` held by `voiceBus.hold(id, 'hold' \| 'once', maxMs)` while she stands on it (VoiceBank `held` cuts the decoded clip, cached per buffer). Dad's buttons open at levels 2–3: a level-2 yes is a hint (`replay`), level 3 asks on the far bank (`say`), a no or `BRIDGE.dadWaitMs` replays. A wrong picture: `bridge_picked`, its units' sounds, `bridge_<where>` (`whereDiffers`) and the stone's sound, back to the near bank. Right: `words` recorded, the level moved once, bag spend, `word_<w>`, into the `Bag`, `episode.read`/`fetched`. Hooks: `room.bridge`. |
| Dad buttons | `src/input/dadButtons.ts` | Item 053. Keys 1 (no) and 2 (yes), live under dad lock, ignored unless a game has `openDadButtons(fn)` open (it returns the closer; close on SHUTDOWN). The DOM cue `#dad-buttons` (bottom right) shows while open, lit by `setDadAsking(true)`. Tests: `__ww.dad('yes')` or `page.keyboard.press('Digit2')`. |
| Cast | `src/rules/cast.ts` | Pure, unit-tested. `drawCast({ hero, recent, overrides }, rand, active = ACTIVE_CAST)`: the dads' forms, the villain (per person from the active cast's villains, avoiding her recent villains), the ally (from the active allies), the primary and secondary dads; `wakerOf`, `seededRand`. Odds: `CAST_ODDS` in config. |
| Sentence game | `src/scenes/SentenceRoom.ts` | A `Minigame` per side room, from `planFor(slot)`. Passes `listen` (skipped when `frameIsSolid`) → `read` → `find` → `done`. Right: prefix line + `word_<key>`, the picture flies into the `Bag`, `progress.record` for `words` and `frames`. Then `backToHub`. |
| Entry hall (warm-up) | `src/scenes/EntryScene.ts`, `src/rules/grab.ts` | Item 033, step `grab` between the bedroom and house part 1. `grabPlan()` (Episode.ts) draws the word first (`pickGrab`: a bank `thing`, her grabs' shakiness × the bank's coverage and level gate, not last episode's, not the gift word, a move pad word or the villain's named counter word), then the house plan (the word goes into `saidBefore`, so no room key, counter or machine word), then the hall's other pictures (`hallObjects`, `distractorsFor` judged by her grabs, clear of every word read later). The clue is `clue(word, level, help)`: `word_<w>` at level 1 or after `GRAB.helpAfter` misses, `letter_<x>_name` letter by letter at levels 1–2, then the sounds by sound unit always (`letter_sh_sound` lights both letters, `span` 2). Right: ProgressStore `record('grabs', ...)` and `setGrabLevel` (`nextLevel`), the word onto `episode.fetched`. Layout is `ENTRY_HALL` in `content/house/house.ts`. |
| House mood | `src/world/houseMood.ts` | `applyHouseMood(scene, villainId, { seed, floor, reveal })`: the villain's `CharacterSpec.house` tint, `decal_<id>` scatter (deterministic per seed, kept off doors and spots) and ambient `fx`. `reveal` is the bedroom's bad-news animation. |
| Bag | `src/ui/Bag.ts` | The backpack, top left. `Bag.contents()` is `pic_<word>` per fetched word, then the counter-item; `receive(art, x, y)` flies a thing in. Draws only; the caller updates `episode`. |
| Minigames | `src/scenes/minigames/Minigame.ts` | Piece meter, `right()` / `wrong()` (no-fail rules), `completePrize()`. |
| Rounds | `src/rules/rounds.ts` | Pure. `soundRound`, `blendRound`, `pictureRound` (parked games), weighted by `ProgressStore.shakiness`. |
| Sentences | `src/rules/sentences.ts` | Pure, unit-tested. `episodePlans` (the rooms' games from `drawGames`, each a frame; three different key words via `drawKey` from her word bag (`BAG_WORDS`, the bank's things, by `drawWords`), never the gift word, a word on any of the episode's move pads (`moveWords`), a word any of the three sentences shows, or anything `saidEarly` by the cast, the quest line or the hub scene (`saidBefore`); the counter word also never a fetched word; the room's other pictures by `distractorsFor`, skipping look-alikes and the other key words), `frameIsSolid` (`SENTENCE_SOLID` in config), three objects per room. `Episode.counterWord/namedCounter/counterArt` read the attic's plan. |
| Moves | `src/rules/moves.ts` | Pure, unit-tested. `drawGift(store, { helper, avoid })`: the gift word, an action word from the move bag by `drawWords`, the helper's own move words weighted double, not spent. `planMoves` (item 018): the episode's four move choices, 2–3 pads each, the gift word first in each part, the other right words from her move bag (ProgressStore `moveBag`, `drawMove`), wrong pads by `distractorsFor` from the open levels; `ownerFor` says who fires each (the gift word: her hero with the helper's move, `giftOwner`; a word a teammate's ability answers to, `abilityWords`: that teammate's styled move; anything else: her hero with the word's own effect, `from: null`). `battle1Words` feeds the counter draw. `Episode.movePlan()` makes it once; `BattleScene.moveChoice` reads it. |
| Word bank | `content/phonics/bank.ts`, `src/rules/bank.ts` | Item 037. `BANK` (each entry tagged by `tag()`: `units`, `level`, `vowel`, `onset`, `rime`), `THINGS` (role `thing`/`both`: rooms, key words, the counter, the warm-up), `ACTIONS` (`action`/`both`: `MOVE_WORDS`, the gift). `wordWeight` = `shakiness` × `coverage` (the vowels, onsets and levels she has met least, `met`) × `levelGate` (level 2 opens as level 1's `firmness` passes `BANK_DRAW.levels[2].start`, and so on); `drawWords` draws by it, shut levels only as a last resort. `distractorsFor`: other first letters while a word is new, one sharing its onset or rime once `isFirm`. `coverageTable` feeds the grown-ups page. |
| Word effects | `content/phonics/effects.ts` | One `WordEffect` per bank word: things `item_<w>` (fetched items, `FETCH_ITEMS`), actions `word_<w>` (a pad cast by her hero, supercharged). `EFFECT_BY_WORD`. |
| Story | `src/rules/story.ts`, `content/house/scenes.ts`, `content/house/quests.ts` | `drawStory(helper, rand, fixed)`: the quest line and hub scene variant numbers (from 1; always 1 for `?cast=default`). `hubStory(helper, narrator, n)` / `questSetup(...)` give the lines and ids (`hub_<owner>_<n>_<key>`, `quest_<owner>_<n>`; a helper without its own uses `ANY_SCENES` / `HOME_QUEST` in the narrator's voice). |
| House data | `content/house/house.ts` | `BEDROOMS` (objects and reactions), `SIDE_ROOMS` (the house's: `FETCH_ROOMS` picks two per episode; `COUNTER_ROOM` is `attic`), `PLACES` (quest places: a hub look, two rooms and a secret room each; `ALL_SIDE_ROOMS`), `HUB` (positions of helper, circle, doors, the same in every place). Art ids derive from it. |
| Quests | `content/house/quests.ts` | `QUESTS` (roster id → place and three setup `lines`), `questFor(id)` (else `HOME_QUEST`, the house and the narrator's `quest_home_<n>` lines). |
| Sentence data | `content/phonics/sentences.ts` | Pure. `FRAMES` (`fetch` / `counter`, slots `{villain}` `{helper}` `{hero}` `{x}`, `{x}` last), `FETCH_ITEMS` (every bank thing's effect from `effects.ts`), `LOOKALIKES`, `sentence()`, and `allPrefixes()` / `allFrameTokens()` that the voice composer reads for `sent_*` and `word_*` lines. |
| Progress | `src/state/ProgressStore.ts`, `src/state/progress.ts` | localStorage key `wordwarriors.progress`, version 1. Kinds `sounds` (letters and the digraphs SH CH TH CK), `words`, `spelling`, `frames`, `rimes`, `onsets`, `grabs` (each added without a bump; old blobs get an empty one); bags `bag` (key words) and `moveBag`; `topUpBag` adds new catalogue words to a round under way and drops words the game no longer has. |
| Battle | `src/battle/BattleScene.ts` | `part1()` / `part2()` are the director scripts; `think()` is the per-fighter AI; `hit()` is the one damage path (health floors live here). `useAllFetched()` fires each fetched item's ability out of her bag. The move choice lays word pads on the floor (`padSpots`, `makePad`); she walks her hero onto one and presses green; the first choice of each part is the gift word. `ownerOf(owner, word)` turns a planned `MoveOwner` into a fighter and ability (with `from: null`, the word's `EFFECT_BY_WORD` ability). |
| Fight styles | `src/battle/stages/`, `content/battle/fights.ts`, `src/rules/fights.ts` | Item 031. `Episode.fightStyle()` draws the showdown's style once (`pickFight`: the villain's `fight`, or the ally's `fightNudge`; `?fight=`). `BattleScene` makes its stage (`makeStage(kind)`) and calls it at every beat; `BaseStage` (the brawl) does nothing, `RaceStage` / `TugStage` / `ClashStage` / `CookoffStage` place the fighters (`steer`), dress the arena, draw their own meter (`drawHud`) and stage the win and lose beats (`ultimate1`, `counter2`, `finish`). `hookState.battle.fight` / `.stage` for the tests. |
| Abilities | `src/battle/abilities.ts` | `CASTS[kind]`; `cast(world, fighter, ability, target, mult, banner)`. |
| Movement | `src/battle/movement.ts` | `STEER[style]`. |
| Fighters | `src/battle/Fighter.ts` | Sprite, statuses, shields, afterimages. |
| Juice | `src/fx/juice.ts` | `burst`, `shockwave`, `shake`, `flash`, `hitstop`, `slowmo`, `timeScale(scene)`. |
| Test hooks | `src/testHooks.ts` | `window.__ww`, dev builds or `?hooks=1` only. |
| Voice build | `tools/voice/compose.ts` → `tools/voice/build.ts` | lines.json from data; audio + manifest; clips override. The game never reads `lines.json`: it reads `public/voice-gen/manifest.json`. |
| Voice import | `tools/voice/import.ts`, `tools/voice/split.ts`, `tools/recorder/phonics-recorder.html` | Item 057. `voice:import -- <zip>`: per card (`processTake`) high-pass, envelope split (`splitTake`, `SPLIT`), strict count (`countReason` says why not), medoid repeat (`rankRepeats`) that passes `holdCheck` (`holdPlan`, loop ≥ `MIN_LOOP_S`), `finish` (fades, -17 dBFS RMS, 30 ms silence), MP3 (`@breezystack/lamejs`); installs `<id>.<hash8>.mp3` through `clipRecord` (`addClip.ts`), unknown ids into `index.json`'s `waiting`. The recorder page ports `SPLIT` / `countTake` / `countReason` between `detector:begin` and `detector:end`; `tests/unit/voiceImport.test.ts` runs both. |
| Line ids | `content/voice/ids.ts` | Every id and narrator key is built here (`wordLine`, `narratorLine(n, moveKey('find_move', w))`, `villainKey`, `wakeCastKey`, `coreLine`, `fightKey`…), by the composer and the game alike: grep a builder, not an id. `voice:lines` only refuses duplicates; `tests/unit/rules.test.ts` checks the ids the game builds exist. Variant counts (`RIGHT_VARIANTS`, `SILLY_VARIANTS`) come from `content/voice/script.ts`. |
| Art | `tools/art/placeholders.ts` | Placeholder PNGs + `docs/art.md`. |
