# Systems: where each thing starts

| System | Entry point | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | `src/main.ts` → `src/scenes/BootScene.ts` | Makes particle textures, loads only the title's art (item 065; the rest on demand, below), reads the voice manifest, handles `?scene=`. |
| Art on demand | `src/art/ArtBank.ts`, `src/art/needs.ts`, `src/art/gate.ts`, `src/scenes/WaitScene.ts` | Item 065. `artBank.ensure(ids)` / `prefetch(ids)`: each `public/art/<id>.png` fetched once (high priority for a scene about to show, the default for the background), decoded and added to Phaser's texture manager; a missing file or a lost fetch gets the magenta square, asked again later by `retryFailed` and swapped in by `applyHeld` once nothing shows it. `artFor(sceneKey, draw)` lists a scene's art from the episode. Every scene starts through `startScene(from, key, data)`: at once when its art is in, else behind the Wait scene (`voiceBank.pauseWhile` meanwhile); each scene's CREATE prefetches the scenes that can follow. An art id read before it loaded warns `art: not ready <id> (<scene>)` (`art-late` in the checker, an error in `collectErrors`). `__ww.art()`: missing textures on screen, magenta ids, late asks, loads in flight. More: `docs/qa-findings.md`, *Systems history*. |
| Title | `src/scenes/TitleScene.ts` | The logo, the roster parading past, one green dot: green (or Start, Space, Enter) unlocks audio and starts the episode. Holding both shoulder buttons (LB+RB) for two seconds, or F9, opens the grown-ups page. While audio is still locked a grown-up note says the sound is off. |
| Grown-ups page | `src/scenes/ParentScene.ts` | F9 anywhere, or LB+RB on the title. Mixed case. Per sound, word and frame: tries, first-try-right rate, tries since the page opened, `+n` solo-mode modelled reads; colour is the shakiness the draws use. Green / Space steps pages 1 (sounds, warm-up, frames, bag, coverage; first sounds `firstSoundTag`, one `vowel` row for ANT and APPLE alike, item 144), 2 (every word in play, the action words too, grey until met, item 144; bridge levels in the dots) and 3 (stored words with their reasons, item 127; the `odd` things); red / Esc / F9 goes back to the scene it came from with its data; F8 twice erases all progress. |
| THE END | `src/scenes/TheEndScene.ts` | Item 085, the last screen of the victory step (Ending → Victory → TheEnd): one of the episode's places, her heroes bouncing, THE END, a fanfare. Only Dad's 2 (`openDadButtons`, its own cue label) goes on: `advance()` ends the episode and opens the title. |
| Episode flow | `src/episode/Episode.ts`, `state.ts`, `draws.ts`, `cast.ts`, `plans.ts` | The step list (bedroom → entry hall warm-up → house 1 → battle 1 → house 2 → battle 2 → victory); `advance(scene)` starts the next step's scene, `enterRoom(scene, slot)` / `backToHub(scene, from)` hand over inside a house step. `Episode.ts` holds the flow and re-exports the rest, so scenes import from it alone: `state.ts` the state (`episode`, `steps()`) and the step-to-scene table (`sceneForStep`, `isStepKind`); `draws.ts` the cast draw and its remembering (`setCastOptions`, `castEpisode`, `rememberCast`, `resetEpisode`); `cast.ts` the cast lookups (`waker`, `helper`, `narrator`, `castId` / `castArt` / `castFace`, `podiumSecond`, `podiumCheer`, `podiumCheerRight` … `villainSpec`); `plans.ts` the other draws (`giftWord`, `movePlan`, `housePlan`, `grabPlan` … `fightStyle`, `journeyStops`). `episode.story` (`drawStory`) gives `questSetupLine()` and `hubScene()`; `storyWords()` keeps their words out of every draw. `episode.fetched` and `episode.gift` feed the battle. Default episode: `EPISODE_DEFAULTS` (`src/config.ts`); parked kinds only by `jumpTo`. None of the four imports `Episode.ts` or Phaser, so the draws run in node (`tests/unit/episodeDraws.test.ts`). More: `docs/qa-findings.md`, *Systems history*. |
| Input | `src/input/Controls.ts` | Create one per scene in `create()`; call `update()` first and `endFrame()` last in `update()`. `virtualPad` is the test input. Keys: arrows, Space/Enter green, Esc red, Tab yellow, 3 (digit or numpad) blue; nothing on Shift (five presses open Windows' Sticky Keys, amendment 1579). The browser's own keys: `installBrowserKeys` (`src/input/browserKeys.ts`, from `main.ts`) prevents those a child mashing the keyboard finds (F1, F3, F6, F7, F10, the menu key, Firefox's quick find), from boot on, and Tab's focus move once the game is up, after Phaser has seen it (hunt 11). |
| Spelling keys | `src/input/TextEntry.ts` | `openTextEntry(fn)` while a spelling scene is open; returns the closer. |
| Voice data | `src/voice/VoiceBank.ts` | `voiceBank.loadGroups([...])`; groups are `core`, `phonics`, `nar_<narrator>`, `fighter_<fighter>`, `end_<person>` (victory scene lines). One decode queue, 12 clips at a time (`DECODE_SLOTS`), a group's background clips at most 4 of them at `low` fetch priority (`BACKGROUND_SLOTS`, item 150: twelve held all six of a host's connections, so a slow server's art and lines queued behind them); a line asked for by `ensure` (about to be said) jumps the queue at `high`; boot awaits only the manifest, core decodes behind the title; a fetch is given up after 15 s of wall-clock time. A room can `ensure` its clips as it opens (the bridge its stones and words). VoiceBus waits at most `ENSURE_TIMEOUT_MS` (4 s) for a clip, then plays the caption alone for the line's estimated length. |
| Wall-clock timeouts | `src/realTime.ts` | `realTimeout(fn, ms)`: a timer on the real clock for waits on real work (a fetch that never answers, `ENSURE_TIMEOUT_MS`), which the tests' fast page clocks (`warpClocks`, `tests/harness.ts`) do not stretch (item 069). In the game it is plain `setTimeout`. |
| Voice arbiter | `src/voice/VoiceBus.ts` | `voiceBus.say(id, 'instruction' \| 'bark', anchor?)`. The only way to speak. Caption and Bubbles listen to it. |
| SFX | `src/audio/sfx.ts` | Synthesised; one master gain, ducked while a voice line plays. |
| Music | `src/audio/music.ts` | Synthesised step sequencer: `startMusic('title' \| 'room' \| 'battle')`, ducked under speech. |
| Audio context | `src/audio/context.ts` | The one `AudioContext` (`getAudioContext`), shared by sfx, music and voice: one unlock to get right (`unlockAudio`, on a press), and its `currentTime` is the clock the word highlight rides on. `holdAudio` / `releaseAudio` hold it while the page is hidden (`main.ts`); VoiceBus waits on `whenAudible()` before a line. |
| Victory scene | `src/scenes/EndingScene.ts`, `src/rules/endings.ts`, `content/house/endings.ts` | The victory step starts here (`sceneForStep`, `src/episode/state.ts`, gives `Ending`), then goes on to `Victory`. `pickEnding(episode, progress.recentOf('ending'))` once per episode (her last `VARIETY.rotations` scenes sit out, item 067) (`episode.ending`; `?ending=<id>` fixes it); `endingPeople(cast)` (the hero first, the villain last) stand across `stand`; `endingScript(cast, id)` is the four beats, each `{ who, line }`, said as barks over `who` (a dropped bark is tried again). `hookState.ending` for tests. Voice groups `end_<person>` are in `episodeGroups()`. |
| Victory recap | `src/scenes/VictoryScene.ts`, `src/rules/recap.ts` | Her reading recap after the victory scene. `recapWords(input, max = 9)`: nine cards fit; when she read more, the gift word, the battles' move words, the counter and what came after it, and each side room's last word always stay, and the earliest of the rest fill the cards left. Unit test: `tests/unit/recap.test.ts`. |
| Sound-out | `src/rules/soundOut.ts` | Pure. `soundOut(word)`: one step per sound unit (a digraph one sound, never its letters), shared by the warm-up's clue, the sentence room's key-word hint, the bridge's wrong pick, Charge the Spell, the Shape-Shifter's wrong picture and the rhyme room's printed word. `soundLines(sound)`: a letter's sound as the Shape-Shifter's hints and misses give it, alone, never the letter's name (item 137). |
| Journey map | `src/scenes/JourneyScene.ts` | Shown by `advance()` before battle and victory steps (`JOURNEY_BEFORE`) and before house part 2; `?nojourney` skips it. |
| Frame safety net | `src/main.ts` | `Phaser.Game.prototype.step` wrapped so one throwing frame cannot stop the loop. |
| Dialogue box | `src/ui/Caption.ts` | Instruction lines (and barks with no anchor, e.g. a taunt heard from afar), spoken word highlighted, beside the speaker's framed face and name on their side with a tail. Top or bottom, whichever covers less of the scene's `avoid()` keep-outs (rooms: `RoomScene.keepOut()`, spots, her, the banner, the scroll); `track(id, where)` puts a face on the side the speaker stands and a glow at their feet; `mode(u)` per line: `show`, `portrait` (face only: the sentence banner and recap cards light their own words) or `hide` (always for the ids `uncaptioned()` in `content/voice/ids.ts` names: `letter_*` and `sent_*`). `compact` in battle: small and always at the bottom. `narrow(u)` (the lines that send her walking): a 320 px box of up to four rows where it covers least, then wider, shorter boxes (`NARROW_WIDER`) while it still covers something. More: `docs/qa-findings.md`, *Systems history*. |

| Bubbles | `src/ui/Bubbles.ts` | Barks over a moving anchor (`{x, y, bubbleOffset, active}`), e.g. a `Fighter`, with a small face chip; the tail follows the speaker even when the bubble is held in from an edge. |
