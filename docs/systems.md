# Systems: where each thing starts

| System | Entry point | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | `src/main.ts` → `src/scenes/BootScene.ts` | Loads every art id in `content/art/assets.ts`, makes particle textures, reads the voice manifest, handles `?scene=`. |
| Episode flow | `src/episode/Episode.ts` | The step list (bedroom → house 1 → battle 1 → house 2 → battle 2 → victory); `advance(scene)` starts the next step's scene. Inside a house step the hub and its rooms hand over with `enterRoom(scene, slot)` / `backToHub(scene, from)`. `castEpisode()` draws the cast (`src/rules/cast.ts`) and the quest place at each episode's start; `waker()` is the dad who wakes her, `helper()` (the ally, or the waker) teaches the spell, fills `{helper}` and leads the quest, `narrator()` is the helper's person (whose voice the instructions are in); `castId(id)` / `castArt(id)` / `castFace(id)` turn a person into the form they play this episode. `housePlan()` makes the episode's three `RoomPlan`s once. `episode.fetched` (key words, in order) and `episode.spell` feed the battle. Cast options from the URL: `setCastOptions` (BootScene `castOptions`); the fixed default episode is `EPISODE_DEFAULTS` (`src/config.ts`). Parked v0.1 kinds (`alarm`, `prep`, `retreat`, `spell`) are reachable only by `jumpTo`. |
| Input | `src/input/Controls.ts` | Create one per scene in `create()`; call `update()` first and `endFrame()` last in `update()`. `virtualPad` is the test input. |
| Spelling keys | `src/input/TextEntry.ts` | `openTextEntry(fn)` while a spelling scene is open; returns the closer. |
| Voice data | `src/voice/VoiceBank.ts` | `voiceBank.loadGroups([...])`; groups are `core`, `phonics`, `nar_<narrator>`, `fighter_<fighter>`, `end_<person>` (victory scene lines). |
| Voice arbiter | `src/audio/VoiceBus.ts` | `voiceBus.say(id, 'instruction' \| 'bark', anchor?)`. The only way to speak. Caption and Bubbles listen to it. |
| SFX | `src/audio/sfx.ts` | Synthesised; one master gain, ducked while a voice line plays. |
| Music | `src/audio/music.ts` | Synthesised step sequencer: `startMusic('title' \| 'room' \| 'battle')`, ducked under speech. |
| Victory scene | `src/scenes/EndingScene.ts`, `src/rules/endings.ts`, `content/house/endings.ts` | The victory step starts here (`SCENE_FOR.victory` is `Ending`), then goes on to `Victory`. `pickEnding(cast, progress.lastEnding(), rand)` once per episode (`episode.ending`; `?ending=<id>` fixes it); `endingPeople(cast)` (the hero first, the villain last) stand across `stand`; `endingScript(cast, id)` is the four beats, each `{ who, line }`, said as barks over `who` (a dropped bark is tried again). `hookState.ending` for tests. Voice groups `end_<person>` are in `episodeGroups()`. |
| Journey map | `src/scenes/JourneyScene.ts` | Shown by `advance()` before battle and victory steps (`JOURNEY_BEFORE`); `?nojourney` skips it. |
| Frame safety net | `src/main.ts` | `Phaser.Game.prototype.step` wrapped so one throwing frame cannot stop the loop. |
| Dialogue box | `src/ui/Caption.ts` | Instruction lines (and barks with no anchor, e.g. a taunt heard from afar), spoken word highlighted, beside the speaker's framed face and name on their side with a tail. Top or bottom, whichever covers less of the scene's `avoid()` keep-outs (rooms: `RoomScene.keepOut()`, spots, her, the banner, the scroll); `track(id, where)` puts a face on the side the speaker stands and a glow at their feet; `mode(u)` per line: `show`, `portrait` (face only: the sentence banner and recap cards light their own words) or `hide`. `compact` in battle: small and always at the bottom. |
| Speaker portraits | `src/ui/Portrait.ts` | `speakerOf(u)` (`phonics` = Dada Matt, or the waker while he is the villain; a line's speaker is a person, and the face is the form they play), `makePortrait(scene, speaker, size)`: the name-free face card (evil: the top of the body art) framed in the speaker's palette colour, name plate under it. |
| Bubbles | `src/ui/Bubbles.ts` | Barks over a moving anchor (`{x, y, bubbleOffset, active}`), e.g. a `Fighter`, with a small face chip; the tail follows the speaker even when the bubble is held in from an edge. |
| Text fitting | `src/ui/fitText.ts` | `fitText(text, w, h?)`: wraps between words, then shrinks until it fits. Every text box that can run long uses it (bubbles, battle banner, `floatText`, recap cards, grown-ups page); the caption and the sentence banner lay out rows of words themselves. `__ww.texts()` lists visible texts with an `offscreen` flag (`tests/text.spec.ts`). |
| Walking rooms | `src/world/RoomScene.ts` | Base for picks, the bedroom, the hub, the parked games. Subclass: `background()`, `build()`, `choose(spot)`, optional `hint()`. A spot with `correct: true` is what F4 walks to. |
| Bedroom | `src/scenes/BedroomScene.ts` | The wake-up. Plays `BEDROOMS[0]` (`content/house/house.ts`): the waker (`waker()`: the primary dad in his form) gives the `wake_*` lines, the secondary dad comes in too (`wake_<dad>_hi` half the time), the helper says the quest's setup line (`questSetupLine()`), the villain's mood reveals, each `BedroomObject` does its `reaction` + `sound` on green, the door sparkles after `DOOR_SPARKLE_MS`. No text, no reading. |
| Hub | `src/scenes/HubScene.ts` | Phases `intro → circle → doors → secret → front → leaving`. Part 1: the spell lesson (`spellFor`) in the magic circle, then doors `a` and `b`; part 2: the secret `counter` door (the attic). A door is invisible until it is usable: the side doors pop in after the spell, the front door when the part's rooms are done, the counter door in part 2. Restarted on each return, so all state is read back from `episode`. Its backdrop is `hubArt(episode.place)` (the house's `room_hub` or a quest place's `room_place_<id>`); away from home the way out is `prop_portal` and the lines are the `away_*` ones. |
| Minigame pool | `src/rules/minigames.ts` | Pure. `MINIGAMES` (id, scene, weight, slots), `drawGames(store, rand)`: two fetch games and a counter game per episode, weighted, no repeat; `enterRoom` starts the drawn game's scene. The sentence game in each frame, and the word machine (fetch rooms only). `episodePlans` (`src/rules/sentences.ts`) turns the draw into `RoomPlan`s (`kind: 'sentence' | 'machine'`, each with `key` and `fetches`); `?game=` / `?games=sentence` override the draw (`roomGames` in Episode). |
| Word machine | `src/scenes/WordMachine.ts`, `src/rules/wordMachine.ts`, `content/phonics/families.ts` | A `Minigame` for a fetch room whose plan is `kind: 'machine'`. `planMachine(store, { avoid, taken })`: `rounds` words from the word bag, one family then another, leaning to shaky `rimes`, `words` × `onsets`. Per round: picture, rime, tiles (`onNear` says the letter sound); green → `letter_<x>_sound`, a beat, `rime_<r>`; right → payoff, `word_<w>`, picture into the `Bag`, `episode.fetched`/`read`, `progress.record` for `words`, `rimes`, `onsets`, bag spend; wrong → `machine_makes` + `silly_<w>` + `machine_silly_N`, the tile pops back. Hooks: `room.machine`. |
| Cast | `src/rules/cast.ts` | Pure, unit-tested. `drawCast({ hero, recent, overrides }, rand)`: the dads' forms, the villain (per person, avoiding her recent villains), the ally, the primary and secondary dads; `wakerOf`, `seededRand`. Odds: `CAST_ODDS` in config. |
| Sentence game | `src/scenes/SentenceRoom.ts` | A `Minigame` per side room, from `planFor(slot)`. Passes `listen` (skipped when `frameIsSolid`) → `read` → `find` → `done`. Right: prefix line + `word_<key>`, the picture flies into the `Bag`, `progress.record` for `words` and `frames`. Then `backToHub`. |
| House mood | `src/world/houseMood.ts` | `applyHouseMood(scene, villainId, { seed, floor, reveal })`: the villain's `CharacterSpec.house` tint, `decal_<id>` scatter (deterministic per seed, kept off doors and spots) and ambient `fx`. `reveal` is the bedroom's bad-news animation. |
| Bag | `src/ui/Bag.ts` | The backpack, top left. `Bag.contents()` is `pic_<word>` per fetched word, then the counter-item; `receive(art, x, y)` flies a thing in. Draws only; the caller updates `episode`. |
| Minigames | `src/scenes/minigames/Minigame.ts` | Piece meter, `right()` / `wrong()` (no-fail rules), `completePrize()`. |
| Rounds | `src/rules/rounds.ts` | Pure. `soundRound`, `blendRound`, `pictureRound` (parked games), weighted by `ProgressStore.shakiness`. |
| Sentences | `src/rules/sentences.ts` | Pure, unit-tested. `episodePlans` (the rooms' games from `drawGames`, each a frame; three different key words via `drawKey` from her word bag, never the spell, a word any of the three sentences shows, or anything `saidEarly` by the cast or the quest's setup line (`saidBefore`); the counter word also never a word on battle 1's move pads or a fetched word; distractors skip look-alikes and the other key words), `frameIsSolid` (`SENTENCE_SOLID` in config), three objects per room. `Episode.counterWord/namedCounter/counterArt` read the attic's plan. |
| Moves | `src/rules/moves.ts` | Pure, unit-tested. `planMoves` (item 018): the episode's four move choices, 2–3 pads each, the spell first in each part, the other right words from her move bag (ProgressStore `moveBag`); `battle1Words` feeds the counter draw. `Episode.movePlan()` makes it once; `BattleScene.moveChoice` reads it. |
| Spell | `src/rules/spell.ts` | Pure. `spellFor(hero, helper)`: the helper's first move word the hero lacks, else its first, else `zap`. |
| House data | `content/house/house.ts` | `BEDROOMS` (objects and reactions), `SIDE_ROOMS` (the house's: `FETCH_ROOMS` picks two per episode; `COUNTER_ROOM` is `attic`), `PLACES` (quest places: a hub look, two rooms and a secret room each; `ALL_SIDE_ROOMS`), `HUB` (positions of helper, circle, doors, the same in every place). Art ids derive from it. |
| Quests | `content/house/quests.ts` | `QUESTS` (roster id → place and setup line), `questFor(id)` (else `HOME_QUEST`, the house and the narrator's `quest_home` line). |
| Sentence data | `content/phonics/sentences.ts` | Pure. `FRAMES` (`fetch` / `counter`, slots `{villain}` `{helper}` `{hero}` `{x}`, `{x}` last), `FETCH_ITEMS` (every CVC key word → battle `AbilitySpec`), `sentence()`, and `allPrefixes()` / `allFrameTokens()` that the voice composer reads for `sent_*` and `word_*` lines. |
| Progress | `src/state/ProgressStore.ts`, `src/state/progress.ts` | localStorage key `wordwarriors.progress`, version 1. Kinds `sounds`, `words`, `spelling`, `frames` (`frames` added in v0.2 without a bump; old blobs get an empty one). |
| Battle | `src/battle/BattleScene.ts` | `part1()` / `part2()` are the director scripts; `think()` is the per-fighter AI; `hit()` is the one damage path (health floors live here). `useAllFetched()` fires each fetched item's ability out of her bag. The move choice lays word pads on the floor (`padSpots`, `makePad`); she walks her hero onto one and presses green; the first choice is `spellMove()`. |
| Abilities | `src/battle/abilities.ts` | `CASTS[kind]`; `cast(world, fighter, ability, target, mult, banner)`. |
| Movement | `src/battle/movement.ts` | `STEER[style]`. |
| Fighters | `src/battle/Fighter.ts` | Sprite, statuses, shields, afterimages. |
| Juice | `src/fx/juice.ts` | `burst`, `shockwave`, `shake`, `flash`, `hitstop`, `slowmo`, `timeScale(scene)`. |
| Test hooks | `src/testHooks.ts` | `window.__ww`, dev builds or `?hooks=1` only. |
| Voice build | `tools/voice/compose.ts` → `tools/voice/build.ts` | lines.json from data; audio + manifest; clips override. |
| Art | `tools/art/placeholders.ts` | Placeholder PNGs + `docs/art.md`. |
