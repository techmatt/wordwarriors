# Systems: where each thing starts

| System | Entry point | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | `src/main.ts` → `src/scenes/BootScene.ts` | Makes particle textures, loads only the title's art (item 065; the rest on demand, below), reads the voice manifest, handles `?scene=`. |
| Art on demand | `src/art/ArtBank.ts`, `src/art/needs.ts`, `src/art/gate.ts`, `src/scenes/WaitScene.ts` | Item 065. `artBank.ensure(ids)` / `prefetch(ids)`: each `public/art/<id>.png` fetched once by `fetch` (not an `<img>` request, which Chromium ranks below the voice bank's fetches and starved), at high priority for a scene about to show (4 at a time, leaving two of HTTP/1.1's six connections to voice; one already in flight for the background is aborted and asked again at high) and the default for the background (2 at a time, so the voice keeps the connections; `low` is held back by Chromium for as long as higher requests keep coming, which starved it), decoded with `Image.decode`, added to Phaser's texture manager; a fetch out 2 minutes is aborted and asked again (3 tries, 0.5 s then 2 s apart since item 093), and a file that is not there, or a fetch lost to the network, gets the magenta square. One lost to the network (M25) is asked again in the background by the next scene to start 3 s or more later that needs it (`retryFailed`, from `startScene` and every `prefetch`), at most twice, one 15 s try each; when it comes, the square's texture is replaced only once nothing shows it (else it is held, `applyHeld`, until a scene starts: never under a live image, whose scale and tweens were worked out for the square). `__ww.art()` also reports the magenta ids (`failed`) and images showing them (`magenta`). The bedroom draws the warm-up's pictures early to prefetch them. `artFor(sceneKey, draw)` lists a scene's art from the episode (the cast in every form, all `prop_*`, the villain's decals, the bag, then the scene's own: plans are walked for words with pictures, `artIn`); `draw: true` makes the plans the scene would have made (grab, house, moves, fight, `drawEnding()`), a prefetch (`false`) uses only those already made. Every scene starts through `startScene(from, key, data)` (Episode's `startStep` / `enterRoom` / `backToHub` / `advance` / `jumpTo`, Ending → Victory): at once when its art is in, else behind the Wait scene (stars round her face, fades in after 150 ms), with the voice queue starting no new background decodes meanwhile (`voiceBank.pauseWhile`: the clips queued behind the pictures past their fetch timeout). On each scene's CREATE the scenes that can follow are prefetched (title → bedroom and every form for the title duel; hub → its rooms, the battle; battle 2 → the victory scene, drawn early). Safety net: `textures.get` / `exists` on an art id not loaded warns `art: not ready <id> (<scene>)` and loads it first; `collectErrors` (tests) and the bug-hunt checker (`art-late`) count that as a bug. `__ww.art()`: missing textures on screen, late asks, loads in flight. |
| Episode flow | `src/episode/Episode.ts`, `state.ts`, `draws.ts`, `cast.ts`, `plans.ts` | The step list (bedroom → entry hall warm-up → house 1 → battle 1 → house 2 → battle 2 → victory); `advance(scene)` starts the next step's scene. Inside a house step the hub and its rooms hand over with `enterRoom(scene, slot)` / `backToHub(scene, from)`. `castEpisode()` draws the cast (`src/rules/cast.ts`) and the quest place at each episode's start; `waker()` is the dad who wakes her, `helper()` (the ally, or the waker) gives the gift word, fills `{helper}` and leads the quest, `narrator()` is the helper's person (whose voice the instructions are in); `castId(id)` / `castArt(id)` / `castFace(id)` turn a person into the form they play this episode; `podiumSecond()` is who stands beside her on the victory podium and at THE END. `episode.story` (`drawStory`, `src/rules/story.ts`, drawn with the cast) picks the helper's quest line (`questSetupLine()`) and hub scene (`hubScene()`); `storyWords()` is every word they say, kept out of every draw. `giftWord()` draws the gift word on first need (`drawGift`, kept in `episode.gift`). `movePlan()`, `housePlan()` (the episode's three `RoomPlan`s) and `grabPlan()` are each made once, in that order of dependence. `episode.fetched` (key words, in order) and `episode.gift` feed the battle. Cast options from the URL: `setCastOptions` (BootScene `castOptions`); the fixed default episode is `EPISODE_DEFAULTS` (`src/config.ts`). Parked v0.1 kinds (`alarm`, `prep`, `retreat`, `spell`) are reachable only by `jumpTo`. `Episode.ts` holds the flow; the state (`episode`, `steps()`) and the step-to-scene table (`sceneForStep`, `isStepKind`) are `state.ts`, the cast draw and its remembering (`setCastOptions`, `castEpisode`, `rememberCast`, `resetEpisode`) `draws.ts`, the cast lookups (`waker` … `villainSpec`) `cast.ts`, the other draws (`housePlan` … `fightStyle`, `journeyStops`) `plans.ts`, all re-exported by `Episode.ts`, so scenes import from it alone. None imports `Episode.ts` or Phaser (item 076), so the draws run in node (`tests/unit/episodeDraws.test.ts`). |
| Input | `src/input/Controls.ts` | Create one per scene in `create()`; call `update()` first and `endFrame()` last in `update()`. `virtualPad` is the test input. Keys: arrows, Space/Enter green, Esc red, Tab yellow, 3 (digit or numpad) blue; nothing on Shift (five presses open Windows' Sticky Keys, amendment 1579). |
| Spelling keys | `src/input/TextEntry.ts` | `openTextEntry(fn)` while a spelling scene is open; returns the closer. |
| Voice data | `src/voice/VoiceBank.ts` | `voiceBank.loadGroups([...])`; groups are `core`, `phonics`, `nar_<narrator>`, `fighter_<fighter>`, `end_<person>` (victory scene lines). One decode queue, 12 clips at a time (`DECODE_SLOTS`); a line asked for by `ensure` (about to be said) jumps the queue; a fetch is given up after 15 s of wall-clock time. A room can `ensure` its clips as it opens (the bridge its stones and words). VoiceBus waits at most `ENSURE_TIMEOUT_MS` (4 s) for a clip, then plays the caption alone for the line's estimated length. |
| Voice arbiter | `src/audio/VoiceBus.ts` | `voiceBus.say(id, 'instruction' \| 'bark', anchor?)`. The only way to speak. Caption and Bubbles listen to it. |
| SFX | `src/audio/sfx.ts` | Synthesised; one master gain, ducked while a voice line plays. |
| Music | `src/audio/music.ts` | Synthesised step sequencer: `startMusic('title' \| 'room' \| 'battle')`, ducked under speech. |
| Victory scene | `src/scenes/EndingScene.ts`, `src/rules/endings.ts`, `content/house/endings.ts` | The victory step starts here (`sceneForStep`, `src/episode/state.ts`, gives `Ending`), then goes on to `Victory`. `pickEnding(episode, progress.recentOf('ending'))` once per episode (her last `VARIETY.rotations` scenes sit out, item 067) (`episode.ending`; `?ending=<id>` fixes it); `endingPeople(cast)` (the hero first, the villain last) stand across `stand`; `endingScript(cast, id)` is the four beats, each `{ who, line }`, said as barks over `who` (a dropped bark is tried again). `hookState.ending` for tests. Voice groups `end_<person>` are in `episodeGroups()`. |
| Journey map | `src/scenes/JourneyScene.ts` | Shown by `advance()` before battle and victory steps (`JOURNEY_BEFORE`) and before house part 2; `?nojourney` skips it. |
| Frame safety net | `src/main.ts` | `Phaser.Game.prototype.step` wrapped so one throwing frame cannot stop the loop. |
| Dialogue box | `src/ui/Caption.ts` | Instruction lines (and barks with no anchor, e.g. a taunt heard from afar), spoken word highlighted, beside the speaker's framed face and name on their side with a tail. Top or bottom, whichever covers less of the scene's `avoid()` keep-outs (rooms: `RoomScene.keepOut()`, spots, her, the banner, the scroll); `track(id, where)` puts a face on the side the speaker stands and a glow at their feet; `mode(u)` per line: `show`, `portrait` (face only: the sentence banner and recap cards light their own words) or `hide` (always for the ids `uncaptioned()` in `content/voice/ids.ts` names: `letter_*` and `sent_*`). `compact` in battle: small and always at the bottom. |
| Speaker portraits | `src/ui/Portrait.ts` | `speakerOf(u)` (a line's speaker is a person, and the face is the form they play; a `phonics` line, Dada Matt's voice, shows his face only when he is in the episode on her side, as the waker, the other dad, the ally or her hero, else no face: `phonicsFace` in `src/rules/cast.ts`), `makePortrait(scene, speaker, size)`: the name-free face card (evil: the top of the body art) framed in the speaker's palette colour, name plate under it. |
| Bubbles | `src/ui/Bubbles.ts` | Barks over a moving anchor (`{x, y, bubbleOffset, active}`), e.g. a `Fighter`, with a small face chip; the tail follows the speaker even when the bubble is held in from an edge. |
| Text fitting | `src/ui/fitText.ts` | `fitText(text, w, h?)`: wraps between words, then shrinks until it fits. Every text box that can run long uses it (bubbles, battle banner, `floatText`, recap cards, grown-ups page); the caption and the sentence banner lay out rows of words themselves. `__ww.texts()` lists visible texts with an `offscreen` flag (`tests/text.spec.ts`). |
| Walking rooms | `src/world/RoomScene.ts` | Base for the bedroom, the entry hall, the hub, Debug, the parked pick and retreat scenes, and (through `SideRoom`, the live rooms through `HouseSideRoom`) every side room's game and the parked v0.1 games. | Bedroom | `src/scenes/BedroomScene.ts` | The wake-up. | Hub | `src/scenes/HubScene.ts` | Phases `intro → circle → doors → secret → front → leaving`.
